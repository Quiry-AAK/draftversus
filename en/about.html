About

What is DraftVersus?

DraftVersus is a free football draft and tactics duel that runs entirely in your browser. The idea is simple: build the best squad from a limited pool, steal your rival's star, protect your own, pick the right tactics and win the match.

There are two modes:

  • Play vs AI — single player; the AI makes its own draft, steal/protect and tactical decisions.
  • Online 1v1 — create a room, share the 4-character code with a friend and duel in real time.

Why DraftVersus?

  • No download, no install, no sign-up — open and play.
  • Matches are simulated in real time; 2D and 3D views with live stats (possession, xG, shots).
  • Realistic match dynamics: fatigue, injuries, cards and pressure.
  • Career progress that builds up as you play, with unlockable formations and philosophies.
  • Available in English and Turkish.

Fictional players and clubs

Every footballer, club and name in the game is fictional. Player names are generated to match a regional origin so squads feel believable, but no licensed identities are used and no resemblance to real people, clubs or organisations is intended.

Technology

DraftVersus is written in plain JavaScript and the match engine runs in your browser. Online matches are synchronised through a lightweight server that only relays messages between the two players.
