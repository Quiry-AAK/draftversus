Frequently Asked Questions

Is the game free? Any hidden costs?

DraftVersus is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, energy timers, paywalls or premium squads. The entire player pool and every mode is open to everyone. The site shows ads to cover its costs; ads appear only on menu and break screens and are never shown during a match or the draft.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no password or registration form; you start playing the moment the page loads. Your career stats (series played, wins, goals) build up automatically in your browser. If you want, you can optionally sign in with Google to continue your progress on other devices — entirely up to you, everything works without signing in.

How do I play online with a friend?

Pick "Play Online" on the home screen. In the "Create Room" tab choose the series format, club name and colour; you get a 4-character room code. When your friend types that code into the "Join Room" tab, you both land in the same room. See How to Play for details.

My room code isn't working — what should I check?

  • Make sure the code is 4 characters and typed correctly (letters and digits can be mixed).
  • If the person who created the room left the screen, the room may have closed; create a new one.
  • Rooms with no opponent close automatically after a while — don't wait too long after sharing the code.
  • Make sure the game is opened over draftversus.com rather than from a local file, otherwise online mode cannot connect.

Does my match end if my connection drops?

No, short drops no longer end the match. On a temporary connection problem (a Wi-Fi hiccup, for example) the game automatically tries to reconnect and your opponent is told you are having connection trouble. If you return within about 45 seconds the match continues where it left off. Closing the tab, however, cannot be recovered.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. It is built to run in phone and tablet browsers; you don't need to install anything. You can add it to your home screen as a shortcut.

What do OVR and potential mean?

OVR (overall) is a position-weighted combination of a footballer's attributes: shooting and pace weigh more up front, passing and dribbling in midfield, tackling and physique at the back. That's why two players with the same OVR are worth different amounts in different positions.

Potential (POT) shows the ceiling a player can reach. A young, high-potential footballer can improve across matches, while older players may decline as the series goes on. In a short series (Bo3) current strength wins; in a long one (Bo5) potential is worth more.

Why does position suitability matter?

Playing a footballer out of position costs you real performance. The game shows this with colours: green is their best position, yellow is workable, red means they will struggle. A lower-rated player in the right position often beats a higher-rated one out of position.

How does the steal/protect duel work?

After the draft, each round you make two decisions: which player you want to steal from your rival, and which of your own you want to protect. Choices stay hidden until both sides lock in. If your rival protected the player you targeted, the steal fails. If it succeeds, one of your own players goes to them in exchange.

What can I do during a match?

You can watch in 2D or 3D and change the speed. You have a limited number of substitutions, and you can adjust formation, mentality and player duties mid-match. Half-time allows tactical changes too. Fatigue, injuries and cards directly affect performance.

How tough is the AI opponent?

The AI makes its own draft picks, steal/protect calls and tactical settings. It watches squad balance and makes substitutions during matches. Its decisions are driven by positional need and player value, not randomness.

Is my data stored?

If you don't sign in, no personal data is collected: language/sound preferences and career stats live only in your browser. If you sign in with Google, your stats are saved to the cloud (Firebase, Frankfurt) tied to your account, and only you can access them. In online matches the server keeps temporary room data to relay messages and deletes it when the match ends. Details are on the Privacy Policy page.

Are the footballers in the game real?

No. All footballers, clubs and names are fictional; any resemblance to real people, clubs or organisations is unintended. Player names are generated to match a regional origin so squads feel authentic without using licensed identities.

I found a bug or have a suggestion — how do I report it?

Write to us using the e-mail address on the contact page. When reporting a bug, mentioning your browser/device and the screen where it happened makes it much faster to fix.
