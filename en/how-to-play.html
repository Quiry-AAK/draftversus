How to Play

DraftVersus is a free football draft and tactics duel played in your browser. No download, no sign-up. Across a series (Bo3 / Bo5) you build a squad against an opponent, steal players, choose tactics and watch every match live.

1 · Lobby

Pick your club name and colour, then choose the series format: best of three (Bo3) or best of five (Bo5). In single player you also name the AI-controlled rival club — the AI makes its own draft, steal/protect and tactical decisions. The series starts the moment you hit "Start Draft".

2 · Draft — build your squad

When it is your turn you pick from candidates for an opened position. Every player has an overall rating (OVR) and a position; try to build a balanced squad for your formation. You can change your formation during the draft.

3 · Duel — steal and protect

After the draft comes the duel phase: you may try to steal a player from your rival's squad while protecting one of your own stars. Protecting the right player matters as much as stealing the best one.

4 · Tactics

Before kick-off set your formation, playing philosophy (attacking/balanced/defensive), pressing and individual player duties. Your opponent's tactics stay hidden until the match begins.

5 · Match — watch live, intervene

Matches are simulated in real time by the engine; watch in 2D or 3D. During the match you have a limited number of interventions: substitutions and tactical/formation changes. Fatigue, injuries, cards and pressure all shape how the game unfolds. You can also make changes at half-time.

6 · Result and development

At the end of the series you see match-by-match scores, the player of the series and the footballers who improved. You can jump straight into a rematch.

Online 1v1 — play with a friend

Every stage of single player (draft, steal/protect, tactics, live match) also applies online — the only difference is that your opponent is a real player rather than the AI. Start from "Play Online" on the home screen.

Creating and joining a room

  • Create a room: in the "Create Room" tab pick the series format, club name and colour → you get a 4-character code.
  • Share the code: send it to your friend however you like (WhatsApp, Discord, anything).
  • Join: your friend types the code in the "Join Room" tab and you both land in the same room.

How an online match flows

  • Turn based and live: draft picks alternate and you both see each move instantly. Duel choices stay hidden until both players lock in.
  • Synchronised start: the live match does not begin until both sides finish their tactics and hit "I'm Ready".
  • One authoritative match: one side (the room creator) simulates the match and streams it to the other, so both screens show the same score and the same events.
  • Connection: if your connection drops briefly, the game reconnects automatically and the match continues where it left off.

Tip: online mode needs the game to be opened over draftversus.com; it will not connect if the file is opened locally. Single player against the AI works without an internet connection.
