Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

DraftVersus ("the site", "the game") respects your privacy. This page explains what data is processed when you use the site and why.

No account required

You do not need an account to play. When you play without signing in, no name, e-mail or similar personal information is requested and no personal data is stored on our servers. The club name you type is used only for the game experience.

Data stored in your browser (localStorage)

Your language preference, sound setting, cookie notice acknowledgement and career statistics (series/matches played, wins, goals, level) are kept only in your own browser. Unless you sign in, they are never sent to us. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Optional sign-in (Google) and cloud sync

If you want to continue your progress on another device, you may optionally sign in with your Google account. This feature uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

  • What is processed: the user ID (UID) created by Firebase, your Google display name and e-mail address, plus the career statistics listed above.
  • Why: only to save your progress and sync it across devices. It is not used for advertising and never sold to third parties.
  • Where it is stored: Google Cloud infrastructure, Frankfurt (Germany) region. Only your own account can access your data; security rules prevent other users from reading it.
  • Deletion: to have your account and cloud record deleted, write to us from the contact page. You can also sign out at any time to stop syncing, and clear your browser data to remove the local copy.
  • Details: see Firebase Privacy and Security.

Online mode

In online 1v1 mode the server keeps temporary room information in order to relay game messages between two players. It is deleted when the match ends or the connection drops; no match history or profile is stored.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to improve the site. It collects aggregate statistics such as page views, approximate location (city level) and device/browser information through cookies. This data does not directly identify you. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising

The site shows ads from third-party networks so that it can stay free. Ad content is supplied by third parties; DraftVersus is not responsible for the content of the sites they link to.

Google AdSense and cookies

  • Google AdSense is used to serve ads on this site.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Ads Settings. Alternatively you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • See Google Advertising Technologies and Privacy for more.

Other ad networks

In addition to (or instead of) AdSense, the Adsterra network may be used. These networks may also use cookies and similar technologies to serve interest-based ads. See the Adsterra Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not break the core game; it only affects remembered preferences and analytics/ad measurement.

Children's privacy

Because the game can be played without signing in, no personal data is collected from children in normal use. The optional Google sign-in is intended for users aged 13 and over; younger users are advised to play without signing in and to use the site under parental supervision. Ad content is supplied by third parties.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when necessary; the current version is always published on this page. For questions, reach us via the contact page.
